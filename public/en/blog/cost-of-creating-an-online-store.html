What is Web Hosting?

Before comparing e-commerce platforms, you need to understand web hosting. All websites need hosting, which provides their presence on the internet and storage space for the thousands of files every website requires. It also provides a constant internet connection allowing visitors to access it.

Famous web hosting companies include 1&1, HostGator, and GoDaddy. Web hosting is generally inexpensive, but its cost varies depending on your needs. Basic e-commerce hosting can start at a few dollars per month, while advanced options can cost thousands.

When using one of these companies, you’ll have access to a file management interface that lets you upload (or modify) your site’s files. You can also install programs for various purposes, such as WordPress for blogging or other software for creating forums and communities.

Types of E-commerce Software

There are three main types of e-commerce software for building your website:

1. Open-source E-commerce Software:

Open source means the software’s code is available for everyone to customize. Open-source e-commerce software is usually free. There are no fees to download it, and businesses are allowed to use and customize it to fit their needs. Magento and the WordPress plugin WooCommerce are examples of open-source e-commerce software.

2. Licensed E-commerce Software:

Licensed software is similar to open source in that you are responsible for installing and updating it. However, it’s not free—your business pays a license fee to use the software. These fees sometimes include access to the source code, allowing for the same kind of custom development available with open-source options.

3. Hosted E-commerce Platforms:

Hosted e-commerce software differs from open-source and licensed options. It offers a more comprehensive platform that includes web hosting and other core features as part of the price. The advantage of this option is that you don’t need to install, maintain, or secure the software yourself. All you have to do is create an account and choose a service plan to build your website.

What Does the Cost of Creating an Online Store Include?

The cost of creating an e-commerce website goes beyond just adding pixels. Let’s look at the key elements that make up the cost of an e-commerce project and what you should get for your money:

  • Wireframes: These planning documents, known as wireframes and mockups, form the foundation of your entire project.
  • Page Layouts and Templates: These ensure your blog posts, product pages, contact forms, and other sections maintain a consistent appearance while serving their specific purposes.
  • Design and Development Stages: The focused manual work of designing and building your site makes up the bulk of a full e-commerce website quote.
  • Content Management System (CMS): The backbone that enables you to manage your website easily, allowing updates without needing a developer. This is a crucial cost component.

Additional Costs for E-commerce Projects

Some web design companies offer additional services that complement site-building solutions. As add-ons, they may not be as comprehensive as those offered by specialized agencies, but the convenience of getting all services affordably from a company like Milaknight is unmatched:

1- Graphic Design Services:

Alongside website design, digital marketing agencies can create brand guidelines, including logos, fonts, and colors aligned with your company’s goals. These are used to create marketing materials online (email templates, banners, infographics) and offline (business cards, flyers). Costs range from $100 to $1,000 per task.

2- Content Marketing:

Creates high-quality content in various formats and promotes it across digital channels to increase site visibility. The company may also review your existing content to determine if it needs improvement or updates. Costs vary depending on the work volume.

3- Search Engine Optimization (SEO):

Improves your website’s ranking by optimizing keywords relevant to your business. Services include on-page optimization, link building, and content creation. Specialized digital agencies charge a monthly fee between $3,000 and $5,000 globally.

4- Secure Sockets Later (SSL):

One of the many security features offered by web design agencies, an SSL certificate encrypts data transmitted from the browser to the website. This is crucial for e-commerce sites and those that request sensitive information from customers. This certificate costs $175 per year, but it is typically part of the cost of opening an online store and is part of most web design packages.

5- Website Development:

Covers the services above, offering comprehensive solutions to meet your website’s needs. This may include plugins, applications, updates, support, and maintenance. Expect to pay between $2,000 and $60,000 per year, depending on the services included.
