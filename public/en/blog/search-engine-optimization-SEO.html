Search Engine Optimization Service

Search engine optimization (SEO) remains one of the most important and effective digital marketing channels. SEO is defined as the art of optimizing web pages, landing pages, and content to rank in search engine results (SERPs) based on relevant terms.

An effective SEO strategy can boost your brand's online visibility and accelerate your growth, but, as with any digital channel, goals are constantly changing.

The introduction of artificial intelligence (AI) algorithms like Google RankBrain and AI-powered search results like Google AI Overviews means marketers have to put even more effort into creating high-quality content that delivers value to consumers.

Optimizing Your Site's Search Engine Visibility

Your site's search engine visibility (also known as "search visibility" or "SEO visibility") is the percentage of traffic a website receives from its ranking in organic search results.

Here's how to improve your site's search engine visibility:

1. Target long-tail keywords.

This is the most powerful SEO strategy. If your site isn't showing up as well as it should, it's likely because you're targeting highly competitive keywords. Competition for long-tail keywords on search engine results pages is usually low, meaning you have a good chance of reaching the top three.

2. Check Your Site's Mobile-Friendly Status

Google now lowers the rankings of sites that aren't optimized for mobile devices. Fortunately, it's easy to find out if Google considers your site "mobile-optimized." WebPageTest will tell you whether or not your site is properly optimized for mobile devices.

3. Optimize your website's page titles and descriptions

Organic click-through rate (CTR) is a key indicator of Google rankings. The higher the CTR, the higher your site's ranking. The question is: How do you attract more visitors to click on your results?

Write titles and meta descriptions that make your results more engaging. Specifically, you want to write titles that grab attention without using click-bait tactics, and you want your meta descriptions to market your content.

By consistently using these two things, you can significantly increase your CTR. This increased CTR can lead to a higher Google ranking, thus improving your site's search engine visibility.

4. Build backlinks from relevant sites

If you want to increase your site's search engine visibility, backlinks are essential in your SEO plan, but not just any backlinks. You should focus on getting links from sites in your niche.

For example, if your site specializes in search engine optimization (SEO), getting a backlink from a site specializing in fitness might slightly improve your ranking, but a link from a site specializing in SEO or digital marketing will have a much greater impact.

5. Optimize Your Content for Dwell Time

Google seeks to rank web pages that people like and how it knows if a particular piece of content is liked by dwell time. Dwell time is one of the many user experience signals Google uses to determine whether a site is relevant to a particular keyword.

For example, let's say someone clicks on your result and then returns to Google after 3 seconds. That's a very low dwell time. If enough people bounce back that quickly, Google will discover that your site isn't delivering what people want, potentially dropping your rankings several places.

But if people spend 3 minutes on your page, that tells Google a completely different story. It tells Google that your content is relevant to that keyword, and they may increase your rankings as a result.

The easiest way to improve dwell time is to make your website pages highly attractive, especially the header, which is the first thing a visitor sees when they arrive on your site, so it's important to grab their attention immediately.
