How to Make Your Website Rank Higher on Google: Proven Strategies for 2025

In today’s digital landscape, ranking high on Google isn’t just a nice-to-have—it’s a necessity. Whether you run a local business, an e-commerce site, or a corporate website, visibility on the first page of Google can drastically increase traffic, leads, and ultimately, sales.

At Milaknight, we help businesses unlock their full digital potential. In this guide, we’ll explain exactly how to make your website rank higher on Google, combining SEO best practices with modern tools and data-backed strategies.

Why Your Google Ranking Matters

Before diving into tactics, let’s clarify why this is so important. Studies show that over 90% of users never scroll past the first page of search results. That means if your site isn’t ranking high, you’re missing out on organic traffic and potential customers every day.

Let’s explore how to get your website higher on Google Search—step by step.

1. Master On-Page SEO

One of the core answers to how to make your website rank higher on Google lies in on-page SEO—optimizing your content, titles, meta descriptions, and internal structure.

Use Your Keywords Naturally

Include key phrases like:

  • how to make your website show up higher on Google
  • how to get website ranked higher on Google

Your target keywords should appear in:

  • Page title (H1)
  • Meta description
  • First 100 words
  • At least one subheading
  • Image alt-text

Optimize Title & Meta Descriptions

Craft compelling titles and meta descriptions that include your keywords and encourage clicks.

2. Create High-Quality, Helpful Content

Google rewards content that is relevant, helpful, and well-written. It uses advanced AI (like BERT and MUM) to understand context. That’s why keyword stuffing doesn’t work anymore—but valuable content does.

Content Tips:

  • Answer search intent clearly.
  • Use FAQs, bullet points, and internal links.
  • Refresh old blog posts with new data.
  • Focus on long-form content (like this one!)
  • Write pillar pages and link to related posts.

Milaknight’s content marketing services focus on building content that answers user questions like:

  • How do I get my website higher on Google?
  • How to make my website rank higher in Google search results?

3. Improve Site Speed & Mobile Performance

Page experience is now a ranking factor under Google’s Core Web Vitals. If your site loads slowly or performs poorly on mobile, it will struggle to rank.

Tips to Improve Site Performance:

  • Use tools like Google PageSpeed Insights and Lighthouse.
  • Compress images without losing quality.
  • Use responsive design for all screen sizes.
  • Minify CSS, JavaScript, and HTML files.
  • Choose fast, secure hosting.

At Milaknight, we optimize website performance for speed, UX, and mobile readiness—key to making your website rank higher.

4. Earn High-Quality Backlinks

Backlinks are links from other sites to yours. They act as votes of confidence in your content. The more quality backlinks you earn, the higher your site can rank.

How to Get Backlinks:

  • Publish original research or data.
  • Guest post on industry websites.
  • Build shareable infographics.
  • Use HARO (Help A Reporter Out) to be featured in articles.
  • Get listed in business directories.

We help clients implement powerful link-building strategies that directly answer the question:

“How to make your website rank high in search engines?”

5. Use Schema Markup

Schema markup is code that helps Google understand your content better. It can lead to rich results like FAQs, star ratings, and sitelinks—making your listing stand out and improving click-through rates.

Types of Schema You Can Use:

  • Article
  • Product
  • Local Business
  • FAQ
  • Breadcrumbs

Milaknight includes schema optimization in every technical SEO package—because small code changes can lead to big ranking results.

6. Optimize for Local SEO

If you’re a local business wondering how to make your website show up higher on Google, then Local SEO is your answer.

Local SEO Best Practices:

  • Create and optimize your Google Business Profile.
  • Include local keywords (e.g., “bakery in Riyadh”) on your site.
  • Get listed in local directories (Google Maps, Yelp, etc.)
  • Encourage customers to leave reviews.

How to get your website higher on Google Search for local terms? Focus on proximity, relevance, and trust — all part of Milaknight’s Local SEO service.

7. Audit & Improve Your Technical SEO

Technical SEO is the foundation of your site’s visibility. Without it, even the best content may not rank.

What to Audit:

  • Broken links (404 errors)
  • Crawlability & indexability
  • Duplicate content
  • Redirect chains
  • HTTPS usage
  • XML sitemaps
  • Robots.txt

Milaknight’s SEO team performs comprehensive technical audits to ensure search engines can crawl, index, and rank your website efficiently.

8. Consistent Updates & SEO Monitoring

SEO isn’t a one-time fix. Google’s algorithm changes hundreds of times per year. To stay ahead, you need to analyze, adjust, and improve constantly.

Track Your Progress:

  • Use tools like Google Analytics, Search Console, Ahrefs, or SEMrush.
  • Monitor keyword rankings regularly.
  • Adapt to changes in competitor strategies.

Milaknight offers ongoing SEO management, so your website doesn’t just rank higher—it stays there.
